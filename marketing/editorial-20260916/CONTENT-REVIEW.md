2. Correct unsupported claims without removing useful topics or changing URLs.
3. Use a team byline only when SPACE actually accepts editorial responsibility;
   do not substitute another invented individual or unverified credential.
4. Align visible attribution, author metadata and Article JSON-LD. Update the
   modification date only when a substantive review is actually completed.
5. Replace the temporary notice with a truthful editorial/review disclosure.
6. Review social-share imagery separately. Existing OG/Twitter/schema image
