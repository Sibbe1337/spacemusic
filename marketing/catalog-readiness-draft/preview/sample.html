Income is not ownership evidence.

A royalty statement can show a reported payment basis without proving that a person owns, controls or can transfer the rights. The customer's declaration remains a declaration until the relevant evidence is checked.

Masters are not publishing.

The sample covers only its defined recording receipts. Musical compositions and sound recordings are distinct works in the US Copyright Office reference. This does not resolve any particular country's ownership or transfer rules.

No readiness score. The important result is the specific unresolved item and what to do about it. Five identifiers being present does not make a catalog “83% sale-ready.” Neither a document count nor a synthetic status badge proves transferability.

04 / Scenario and next steps

An assumption is useful.
Only if it stays an assumption.

For illustration only, apply an arbitrary annual-income multiple of 4 to 50% of the already-defined interest represented by the synthetic receipts. Four is not a market benchmark, recommendation or offer multiple.

€12,000 / year×4 assumed×50% share=€24,000

Same arithmetic: €1,000 monthly average × 12 × 4 × 50%. No additional ownership haircut, extra year multiplier or predicted range. Taxes, transaction costs, balances, contract restrictions and payment conditions are excluded. The hypothetical figure is not net cash at closing.

Three actions before a transaction discussion

  1. Close the document gaps. Request Recording F's identifier and signed split evidence. Ask the appropriate adviser about transfer restrictions and any outstanding obligations.
  2. Explain the income window. Retain the original source files. Check reporting lag and context for the quarter difference; do not infer an audience trend from receipts alone.
  3. Choose the conversation you actually need. Use the record with your own adviser or another buyer. If you want SPACE to consider a purchase, request the separate free review. No report purchase is required.

Scope limits. A future real report must be signed off by a named reviewer against the accepted evidence set. It is not legal, tax or investment advice, a certified appraisal, a fraud clearance or a promise that anyone will buy. SPACE's commercial interest must remain disclosed.
