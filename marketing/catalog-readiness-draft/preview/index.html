Clear boundaries

Before you
pay anything.

Is this an independent valuation?

No. SPACE is also a potential buyer of music rights. The proposed report organizes and explains supplied evidence; it would not give an independent market appraisal, certify ownership or replace legal advice.

Why pay when the purchase review is free?

Only if you want a separate, reusable deliverable. A free review answers whether SPACE may consider buying rights. If that is all you need, use the free route. Buying a report would not be necessary.

What if my records are incomplete?

We would confirm the scope first and explain missing inputs. We would not invent income, fill missing months with zero or mark unproven rights as verified. If the agreed work cannot be delivered after payment, a refund or explicitly agreed alternative would be offered.

Will the report tell me my sale price?

No. Any calculation would state its assumptions and would not be a market benchmark or offer. An actual transaction requires individual assessment, agreed rights, terms and a willing buyer.

Can I buy it now?

Not yet. This is a private concept. The owner still needs to approve the service, capacity, customer terms, privacy arrangements and payment setup. No real documents are accepted here.

SPACE is a direct catalog buyer. This optional service is a proposal, not an independent valuation or a purchase offer. The existing free review stays free.
