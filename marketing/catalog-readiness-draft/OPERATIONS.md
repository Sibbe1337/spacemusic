# Delivery and pilot playbook

Draft, 9 September 2026. Proposed service, not current operational capability.

## One deliverable with a bounded scope

One English Catalog Readiness Report for one defined catalog slice: up to 25
master recordings, 12 consecutive complete statement months, one distributor,
one currency and one account/payee. Pilot accepts one agreed machine-readable
CSV export (or one consolidated CSV with source-period references), plus a track
list and a short customer declaration. Cap normalized data at 10,000 rows.
Do not advertise universal distributor support before format review.

Documents are inventoried, not legally certified. Limited document-presence
checks may include a distribution agreement and split evidence. Contract
interpretation, chain-of-title verification, publishing accounting, tax advice,
accounting assurance, royalty recovery and deal negotiation are excluded.
Do not accept passwords, platform login credentials, audio files or ID documents.

The deliverable is a readable PDF, a reconciled aggregate worksheet/CSV and an
action list. Include one correction round for mistakes in the agreed input set,
requested within 14 days of delivery. Do not use this administrative correction
window to limit statutory rights. New evidence or extra catalogs require a new
scope, agreed before charging anything further.

Proposed target: five business days after both cleared payment and confirmed
complete inputs, subject to approved capacity. Missing inputs pause the target;
send a specific list and a revised date. This is not the existing purchase-offer
timeline and must not imply a guaranteed bid.

## Intake before payment

1. Explain free buyer review versus the optional report. If the person only wants a SPACE offer, direct them to the existing free Apply.
2. Confirm business/consumer status and country without assuming a producer is a business. Apply approved tax and consumer rules.
3. Confirm source format, months, currency, recording count and intended use. Review a minimized schema/header example first through the approved channel; do not collect a full catalog just to quote scope.
4. Confirm capacity, total price, precise inclusions, exclusions, timing trigger, privacy terms and cancellation route in a durable message.
5. Only then provide an approved payment link or customer-specific invoice. No charging for a known unsupported format.

Reject or rescope mixed currencies, unallocated deductions, PDF-only/scanned
statements, multiple payees, publishing/master mixtures and unresolved source
totals. Do not invent missing values to fit the package. If unsupported input
is discovered after payment, offer a full refund or a clearly agreed alternative;
never silently deliver a partial report as a full one.

## Delivery workflow and time budget

| Step | Proposed budget | Required output |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Scope and input inventory | 10 min | Opaque report reference; accepted files and periods; exclusions agreed. |
| Normalize and reconcile | 20 min | Line IDs, currency and basis retained; source total ties; duplicates investigated. |
| Interpret the supplied evidence | 15 min | Trends, concentration, missing documents and their limits. |
| Write findings and next steps | 15 min | Every finding has evidence, implication and proportionate action. |
| Second-person quality check | 10 min | Arithmetic, scope, claims, spelling and privacy reviewed by a named person. |
| Deliver and record | 5 min | Accessible PDF, summary CSV, next step and private delivery record. |
| Total target, not measured | 75 min | Support/rework must be timed in addition. |

The operator must complete a dry run before selling. The sample here is not a
timed human delivery. No named first or second reviewer has been confirmed.
An internal time cap does not justify truncating a paid customer's agreed work.
Pause new orders instead, honor the accepted scope or arrange a refund.

## Calculation and data-quality contract

- Keep integer cents and an explicit ISO currency. No implicit FX conversion.
- Identify statement month versus earning/usage month. Describe payment lag. Compare like periods only.
- Confirm whose economic interest the amounts represent. Never multiply the owned share again if the input already represents that share.
- Reconcile the exact reviewed source total to monthly, track and platform totals. Retain signed adjustments; do not delete negative rows.
- Distinguish missing months from explicit zero activity. Do not annualize incomplete history as if it were a full year.
- Identify duplicates with issuer/source references; a repeated amount is not automatically a duplicate. Preserve provenance for corrections.
- A quarterly comparison is a change between those quarters, not year-over-year growth or a forecast.
- Calculate concentration only when the allocation is complete and its denominator is positive. Net reversals can make simple shares misleading; withhold such a chart and explain.
- Never infer ownership, fraud clearance, transferability, tax liability or cash paid out from a statement alone.

For any hypothetical sale illustration, show all assumptions, the income basis,
the share of the already-defined interest and excluded costs. A multiple is an
arbitrary scenario input, not an appraised multiple, legal term or SPACE offer.

## Acceptance checklist for every real report

- Identity of reviewer, review date, version, scoped catalog and evidence inventory are recorded privately.
- Source total and every breakdown reconcile; unsupported parts are explicitly excluded.
- Each material finding cites a file/row/period or is labeled declared/not supplied.
- The reader can tell the next action, its reason and who should perform it.
- No fabricated market data, customer success, testimonial, credentials or automated verification.
- SPACE's buyer interest is disclosed; the report does not pretend to be independent advice.
- Customer can use the report without selling to SPACE. No acquisition consent bundled with purchase.
- Private delivery destination is checked; no public URL, analytics payload or repository contains customer data.
- PDF text, links, page breaks and the underlying calculations pass quality review.

## Commercial experiment

After approvals, first show the sample and scope to five to ten appropriate,
permissioned prospects. Ask what they currently do, what this would replace,
which finding is useful and whether they would actually purchase it at the
stated total price. Do not reinterpret polite interest as willingness to pay.

Sell no more than five accepted pilot orders at the owner-approved price. No
paid traffic, countdown, made-up discount or automatic upsell on Apply. The
five-order cap is an operational limit, not a fake scarcity claim.

Track each invitation, response, paid order, refund, delivery, correction and
all labor minutes separately. A private record template is `pilot-register.csv`;
it has headers only, no invented customers. Keep its populated version outside
this repository in approved access-controlled storage.

Primary economic measure: paid revenue excluding applicable tax, less actual
fees, actual refunds, labor and allocated delivery costs. The preview's refund
reserve is for planning and must not be deducted again on top of actual refunds.
Also report median/range of minutes, timeliness, useful-findings feedback and
whether the customer would recommend or pay for another report. With five
orders, describe counts; do not claim statistical proof or a conversion uplift.

Continue only if customers value the report independently of a bid, accepted
work is deliverable, and real contribution is acceptable to the owner. Proposed
decision threshold: at least three paid deliveries, no material calculation
error and positive total contribution after all pilot labor. This is a business
gate, not market validation. If it fails, revise price/scope or stop.

Existing free-review submissions and qualified catalog opportunities remain a
separate funnel. Do not optimize paid-report revenue at the cost of the core
acquisition business without an explicit owner decision.
