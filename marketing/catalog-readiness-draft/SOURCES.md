## Commercial assumptions, not factual claims

The proposed price, 25-track scope, 12-month window, five-order pilot, five-business-day
target, staff time, hourly labor cost, payment-fee buffer and refund reserve are
internal design assumptions. No customer willingness-to-pay study has been conducted.
No market multiple, genre premium, guaranteed sale price or revenue growth claim is used.

The existing repository contains valuation code with internal weights and risk
thresholds. This draft does not reuse them as commercial truth and does not claim
that the backend, an AI analyzer, a Spotify import or seller workspace is live.

## Evidence labels used in reports

**Observed:** a value or document appears in the supplied material; cite the file,
period and rows. This does not authenticate the material with its issuer.
**Declared:** supplied by the customer but not corroborated by the documents.
**Not supplied:** the required evidence was not in the reviewed set.
**Hypothetical:** a stated assumption or synthetic training example, not an observation.

Never promote a declaration into ownership verification. Do not use a numeric
readiness score, 'verified' badge or valuation range without a defensible methodology.
