# Competitive content gaps: SPACE

Research date: 2026-09-16. Status: read-only research, not approved copy or a publish plan.

## Decision summary

SPACE does not primarily lack English topic coverage. Its 141-URL production
sitemap already includes the core seller guides, valuation, calculator,
comparisons and funding education. The immediate gap is trustworthy commercial
content, followed by an appropriately localized path to the existing free review.

SPACE's approved positioning is a direct buyer of revenue-generating catalogs
across genres, for artists, producers, independent labels and catalog owners.
Apply starts a free review; the calculator models scenarios. Neither is a
guaranteed offer, loan, royalty advance or available report product.

Reviewed five competitors: Duetti, Limbo Music, Royalty Exchange, beatBread and
ANote Music. Public offers differ enough that a single ranking by payout is not
defensible. Private proposals and acceptance were not tested.

## Five prioritized gaps

### 1. P0: Correct commercial claims before translating or expanding

**Observed:** The current release's `/sell/` still includes cinematic-ambient
"Highest Multiples", a "$100 Billion" sleep-market headline, a "$60 Billion"
wellness headline and repeated unsupported buyer-demand assertions. The new
all-genre introduction does not substantiate those statements. The Royalty
Exchange comparison still contains old niche-only SPACE positioning and
unsupported universal speed/control claims. The buyer roundup repeats related
claims. Exact examples and routes are in `existing-coverage.md`.

Royalty Exchange's current [seller FAQ](https://royaltyexchange.com/resources/creators/frequently-asked-questions-about-selling-royalties)
also contradicts the old comparison's treatment of partial sales and fixed
commission. This is a substantive factual correction, not a cosmetic translation
or design task.

**Recommendation:** Put `/sell/`, affected genre copy, the existing Royalty
Exchange comparison, and the buyer roundup through an evidence review first.
Retain useful URLs. Separate sector size, listening demand, documented catalog
income and acquisition pricing; none proves the others. Remove or qualify
unsupported claims after approval. Use the already-corrected Duetti comparison's
dated, first-party, no-universal-winner approach.

**Overlap / new:** Refresh existing pages; no new generic buyer list or selling
guide. Highest priority because translation otherwise multiplies the defect.

### 2. P1: Make seller fit clear without inventing approval rules

**Observed:** [Duetti](https://www.duetti.co/what-we-do) and
[ANote](https://www.anotemusic.com/music-creators) publish explicit screening
guidance; SPACE's Apply explains individual assessment and accepts unknown
income at first contact. Much useful evidence preparation already exists, but
the broad Sell page is dominated by genre cards rather than seller-fit answers.

**Recommendation:** Improve the existing `/sell/`, `/how-it-works/` and Apply
context with an owner-approved fit matrix: existing earning recordings; the
interest actually controlled; documentation available versus requested later;
co-owner/label/distributor commitments; selected tracks versus a whole catalog;
unknown figures versus no revenue. Link to the existing document checklist and
valuation guide instead of restating them. State that submission is not approval.

**Overlap / new:** Existing-page modules and links, not a second eligibility or
valuation guide. A published competitor floor does not establish a SPACE floor,
and an absent SPACE floor does not mean all small catalogs are accepted.

### 3. P1: Test a localized seller journey, not a translated content factory

**Observed:** [Limbo's Spanish seller page](https://www.limbomusic.com/es/sell-your-music-catalog)
is both directly accessible and returned for a Spanish seller query.
[ANote French](https://www.anotemusic.com/fr/music-creators) and
[Italian](https://www.anotemusic.com/it/music-creators) are genuine commercial
seller pages. SPACE's sitemap has no localized paths; all 139 sitemap HTML pages
declare English and none contains an `hreflang` attribute.

**Recommendation:** Spanish is a defensible first candidate on observed content
and intent, not a proven highest-volume market. Compare it with FR, IT, PT-BR,
DE and SV using the parent's actual Google Ads Planner findings and supported
operational languages. After gap 1 is resolved, test one reviewed seller landing
page, process explanation and clearly disclosed Apply handoff. Translate the
actual journey and notices only where support exists; do not imply local offices,
local legal expertise, supported currencies or country eligibility from language.

**Overlap / new:** A genuinely new locale path, provisionally `/es/vender-catalogo-musical/`,
can adapt the corrected Sell intent. No route or translation is approved. Do not
translate all 141 URLs, all 38 genre pages, or US tax claims by default.

### 4. P1: Replace unverified proof with documented seller evidence

**Observed:** [Duetti's artist page](https://www.duetti.co/artist-partners),
[Royalty Exchange's creator page](https://royaltyexchange.com/instant-offer), and
[beatBread's artist stories](https://www.beatbread.com/knowledge-center) make
named evidence easy to find. These remain company-published claims, not audited
proof. SPACE already has a case-study URL, but the internal editorial review
explicitly requires evidence and permission for its purported transaction.

**Recommendation:** Resolve the existing case before using it in new languages.
If permissioned records exist, show the date, role, genre, precise interest sold,
income period, costs, conditions, elapsed stages and what the seller retained.
If records do not exist, use a plainly labelled hypothetical teaching example
or neutral review-process explanation after editorial approval. Do not invent a
customer or imply that a hypothetical case happened.

**Overlap / new:** Repair the existing case first. A new cross-genre case is
conditional on real evidence, not an automatic new-page task. Do not call an
anonymized or named story verified merely because a competitor publishes one.

### 5. P2: Sharpen label and producer commercial entry points

**Observed:** [beatBread](https://www.beatbread.com/label) has a dedicated label
entry point and a separate [rights-holder page](https://www.beatbread.com/other-rights-holders).
[Limbo](https://www.limbomusic.com/sell-your-music-catalog) distinguishes artists,
estates and labels. SPACE's existing funding article already covers label cash
flow, the label's own interest and consent questions; a second funding article
would repeat it. Current persona routes remain largely genre/life-stage based.

**Recommendation:** First strengthen the existing independent-owner page's label
and producer routes. Consider a dedicated commercial `/for/independent-record-label/`
only if Planner/lead evidence and a distinct brief justify it: sell specified
existing label interests, identify artist/producer shares and assignment limits,
exclude future roster commitments explicitly in the agreement, and request the
same free catalog review. A producer page needs the same distinct-intent test;
it must not accidentally attract beat-license or new-song submission intent.

**Overlap / new:** Improve existing routing first. A label acquisition landing
page is a conditional new commercial page; the funding-options article stays
the educational destination. Do not advertise label loans or advances from SPACE.

## Exclusions and guardrails

- No inferred traffic, backlinks, keyword volume, ranking position, conversion lift or market share.
- No claim that SPACE pays more, closes faster, accepts every genre/catalog, retains all creative control, or guarantees a multiple.
- Do not call beatBread a conventional loan or permanent buyout. Do not conflate Limbo advances with acquisitions.
- No investor/passive-income/tokenization cluster: wrong side of the transaction for SPACE's acquisition objective.
- No new generic selling, valuation, documents, offer-checklist, partial-sale, loan-comparison or label-funding pages without a differentiated intent.
- No assumption that publishing-only interests, future releases, estates, particular countries or currencies are accepted merely because a competitor discusses them.
- No broad "sell music online", beat marketplace, sync-library submission or unreleased-song campaign disguised as catalog acquisition.
- No local tax/legal promises, translated US-specific advice, borrowed testimonials, unsupported company scale, or claimed customer case without documentation.
- No live-code edits, draft pages, deployment, paid products or submissions were made.
