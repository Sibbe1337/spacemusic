# Five competitor notes

Observed 2026-09-16. Public first-party statements only. Sources are preserved in
`raw/first-party-sources.md`. "Not observed" means absent from the sampled pages,
not absent from the company. No commercial application or private quote was tested.

## Duetti: direct acquisition comparator

**Offer and fit, observed:** Buys master, publishing and royalty interests from
independent creators, including earnings interests where the seller does not
control the master. Its current offer page states $2,000 annual music-rights
income and tracks as young as six months. This is published screening guidance,
not automatic acceptance. Partial transactions and catalog services are part of
its public positioning. [Offer](https://www.duetti.co/what-we-do)

**Process and conversion, observed:** Meeting/quote entry, deal customization,
then payment; the homepage advertises payment in as little as 30 days. Treat
timing as provider marketing subject to actual conditions, not a tested service
level or basis to assert SPACE is quicker. [Homepage](https://www.duetti.co/)

**Proof and resources, observed:** Named testimonials include A$AP Ant, Ingrid
Contreras and Calimossa. The artist page claims 1,100+ creators; the extracted
page also contains suspicious repeated attributions at its end, so it is not a
clean proof dataset. [Artist partners](https://www.duetti.co/artist-partners)
The [2025 economics report](https://www.duetti.co/2025-music-economics-report)
and [H2 2026 finance index](https://www.duetti.co/music-finance-index-h2-2026)
provide research-led resources; the latter is panel opinion rather than a price
schedule. Its full-report form was not submitted.

**Language, observed:** English on the reviewed seller and resource pages. No
localized seller URL confirmed by the targeted language searches. Global artist
coverage does not prove a localized funnel or supported service language.

**Implication, recommendation:** Improve seller qualification and evidence on
SPACE's existing pages. Its Duetti comparison is already substantially refreshed;
do not recommend creating another one or copying Duetti's eligibility threshold.

## Limbo Music: distribution plus investor-partner acquisition

**Offer and fit, observed:** The sale landing page addresses artists, estates and
labels; masters and publishing; single tracks through catalogs; and full/partial
interests. It presents a free confidential valuation and illustrative pricing
logic. No fixed minimum revenue qualification was found there. The funding
overview says professional investor partners provide funding/acquisitions, so
calling Limbo an identical balance-sheet direct buyer would be imprecise.
[Sale](https://www.limbomusic.com/sell-your-music-catalog),
[funding](https://www.limbomusic.com/funding)

**Process and conversion, observed:** Public explanation leads to an external
valuation/contact form. The acquisition counterparty, terms and acceptance must
be confirmed. Its separate advance product is explicitly exclusive to distribution
clients; do not transfer that condition to sales without evidence.
[Advances](https://www.limbomusic.com/royalty-advances)

**Proof and resources, observed:** The sampled sale page lacks a named completed
Limbo acquisition case. The linked [catalog-selling explainer](https://www.limbomusic.com/blog-posts/what-does-it-mean-to-sell-your-music-catalog)
uses reported celebrity deals as market education, not its own transaction proof.
The page's tax/market generalizations are not validated by this research.

**Language, observed:** English and Spanish seller/funding pages, with an EN/ES
switch. [Spanish sale](https://www.limbomusic.com/es/sell-your-music-catalog),
[Spanish funding](https://www.limbomusic.com/es/funding)

**Implication, recommendation:** A useful Spanish funnel benchmark and later
branded-comparison candidate. Borrow the separation of products and buyer
questions, not its tax claims, multiples or implication that a sale always pays
more. SPACE already mentions Limbo in two relevant existing articles.

## Royalty Exchange: marketplace comparator, not auction-only

**Offer and fit, observed:** Facilitates royalty sales, including the seller's
owned portion and partial revenue streams. Its FAQ describes fixed-term,
life-of-rights and fixed-return arrangements; many transactions concern income
without copyright transfer. No fixed minimum earnings floor was established in
the reviewed seller FAQ. Fees are agreed before listing and collected after an
offer is accepted; a universal 15% charge is not supported there.
[Seller FAQ](https://royaltyexchange.com/resources/creators/frequently-asked-questions-about-selling-royalties)

**Process and conversion, observed:** Review/account connection, valuation and
deal strategy, investor competition, then accepted terms and closing. The
creator page supplies linked transaction cards showing rights type, term, price
and multiple. These are provider-published examples, not comparable SPACE quotes.
[Creator offer](https://royaltyexchange.com/instant-offer)

**Proof and resources, observed:** Jeff Wells' testimonial is linked to an asset
page; that destination returned only an app shell, so the underlying transaction
was not verified. The public [creator hub](https://royaltyexchange.com/resources/creators)
supports seller education, distinct from investor education. Its
[catalog explainer](https://royaltyexchange.com/blog/music-catalog) surfaced in
the English seller search sample.

**Language, observed:** English in reviewed pages. Targeted Spanish search did
not confirm a localized seller page; this is not proof of English-only service.

**Implication, recommendation:** Correct the existing SPACE comparison, then
maintain it alongside the already better-qualified alternatives article. Ask
about the specific marketplace route rather than presenting every deal as an
auction. Do not assert broad artist-name associations prove whole-catalog sales.

## beatBread: advance alternative

**Offer and fit, observed:** Masters/publishing advances for artists, labels and
other rights holders, with configurable cash-flow/term choices and ownership
retention positioning. [Homepage](https://www.beatbread.com/)
The FAQ gives indicative qualification signals, including sustained roughly
10,000 monthly listeners and distributor income; it expressly avoids hard
approval lines. The stated $278 plus 2.8% fee is recouped from future revenues.
The FAQ says advances are limited revenue purchases, not loans, and collection
can continue when a balance remains at term end. Confirm the actual agreement.
[FAQ](https://www.beatbread.com/faq)

**Process and conversion, observed:** Separate [artist](https://www.beatbread.com/artist-funding),
[label](https://www.beatbread.com/label) and
[rights-holder](https://www.beatbread.com/other-rights-holders) routes. Labels
are presented with use cases such as roster growth, new projects and operations.
These are beatBread offers, not SPACE products.

**Proof and resources, observed:** [Knowledge Center](https://www.beatbread.com/knowledge-center)
includes named creator stories, including a linked Madison Rose page. Its
[comparison entry](https://www.beatbread.com/deal-comp-form) offers analysis of
different proposals and a named label testimonial. No comparison tool or offer
was tested. An [anti-impersonation article](https://www.beatbread.com/knowledge-center/learn-more/how-to-spot-and-avoid-scammers-posing-as-beatbread-)
provides a relevant trust pattern.

**Language, observed:** English pages and an EN label were visible; no working
non-English seller path was confirmed. Staging/development search results were
excluded from the offer assessment.

**Implication, recommendation:** SPACE already has a detailed, sourced beatBread
article covering fee arithmetic, recoupment versus term and comparable proposals.
Link to it. The net-new lesson is persona routing and verified trust, not another
"advance vs sale" post or a fabricated SPACE comparison service.

## ANote Music: European royalty-interest marketplace comparator

**Offer, fit and process, observed:** The creator page requires five years of
royalty history, an average EUR 10,000 annual income over that period, and most
earnings from songs at least five years old. Necessary transfer rights must be
verified. Its process is diligence, valuation, auction and payment after success.
It describes temporary/permanent and partial listings, generally minority stakes
for active managers, and a predetermined success fee without upfront listing
costs. Eligibility is not guaranteed auction success. Named testimonials include
Andreas Oberg, Logan Mader and Sterling Fox.
[Creator page](https://www.anotemusic.com/music-creators)

**Proof and resources, observed:** Seller testimonials are public; the
[catalogue page](https://www.anotemusic.com/music-catalogues) directs users into
the platform rather than exposing a full public listing dataset in the fetched
text. The [homepage](https://www.anotemusic.com/) is mainly investor-oriented.
Do not use its investment yield or investor numbers to prioritize SPACE seller
content. Support and news content are available in multiple languages.

**Language, observed:** EN, [FR](https://www.anotemusic.com/fr/music-creators) and
[IT](https://www.anotemusic.com/it/music-creators) seller pages confirmed, plus
French and Italian support results. A German-path retrieval failed; no German
absence claim follows.

**Implication, recommendation:** Clear evidence that seller localization is not
an uncontested market. A later ANote comparison can address deal structure and
fit, not declare SPACE superior below ANote's threshold. Do not advertise an
ANote-style auction, buyback option or future-release funding as a SPACE service.
