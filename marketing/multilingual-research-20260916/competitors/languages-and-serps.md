## Opportunity interpretation

**Observed:** The reviewed competitors use seller-specific pages, process
explanations, qualification, and visible evidence rather than only generic music
education. ES, FR and IT competition is demonstrably localized. PT/DE have
additional localized commercial participants outside the five full profiles.

**Recommendation:** Choose a first language by combining actual country-level
commercial intent, achievable support, conversion evidence and reviewed source
copy. ES is a reasonable content-evidence candidate; FR/IT are clear follow-up
candidates, not automatically lower-volume languages. There is insufficient
evidence here to rank all six languages economically.

**Pre-translation gate:** Correct unsupported `/sell/` genre-market and
highest-multiple claims, stale comparisons and unverified case material before
localizing. Avoid introducing a new claim merely to make translated copy sound
more confident. A localized free review CTA must not become a guaranteed quote,
advance application or report download.

**Implementation is not authorized here:** Route and locale design, translated
form/confirmation/privacy handling, canonicals/hreflang, native copy review and
analytics should be scoped later. First-page success means qualified review
requests and usable documents, not raw traffic or unrelated funding enquiries.
