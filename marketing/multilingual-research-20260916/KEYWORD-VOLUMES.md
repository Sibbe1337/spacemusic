# Keyword demand: verified observations

Research date: 2026-09-16. This is a transcription of authenticated Google Ads
Keyword Planner results, not a downloaded vendor CSV. The CSV download was
blocked by the browser; no security setting was changed or bypassed.

## Measurement contract

- Tool: [Google Keyword Planner](https://ads.google.com/aw/keywordplanner/home), Discover new keywords.
- Historical window: **2025-09 through 2026-08**, explicitly checked in the date selector.
- Network: **Google**, not Google and search partners.
- Location: one country at a time, as shown below.
- Language picker: English, German, Swedish, French, Spanish or Portuguese for the corresponding batch. This is a research setting, not proof that every counted searcher speaks that language. Google's historical average includes searches for the keyword regardless of language.
- Metric: displayed average monthly searches. Values are rounded historical estimates including close variants, not unique people, prospective sellers, traffic forecasts or SPACE impressions.
- Do not sum overlapping phrases into a market size. An information/news query is not automatically a seller.
- The displayed competition column concerns **advertisers**, not organic SEO difficulty. SEO keyword difficulty was unavailable: Semrush returned insufficient API units.
- A displayed dash means **no numerical estimate reported**, not zero searches. A row not captured is separately marked; it is not a dash finding.
- No campaigns, budgets, bids, billing settings or paid-tool subscriptions were changed.

Method reference: [Google's explanation of Keyword Planner statistics](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3022575?hl=en).

SEO difficulty, competitor traffic estimate or qualified-lead forecast is included.
