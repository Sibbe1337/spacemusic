Query batches are discovery evidence, not per-query ranking measurements.
- A translated provider page proves language availability and commercial vocabulary, not the provider's domicile, local search volume, or local customer acquisition. Country evidence is identified separately below.
- This worker's web/API pass did not establish country-specific keyword volume/KD. No estimates, SERP counts, competitor revenue, streaming figures, or music-industry totals are substituted for keyword metrics. Parent owns the separate quantitative dataset.
- Parent subsequently supplied Germany/German and Sweden/Swedish Google Ads Keyword Planner observations, summarized in a distinct addendum below. They are not imported into the Semrush matrix. ## Spanish: Spain and Mexico

**Primary cluster:** `vender catálogo musical` and `venta de catálogo musical`. limbo has an explicit catalog-sale page and a separate advance-versus-sale explanation; Symphonic's Spanish NEXT page distinguishes acquisition from recoverable advances. This validates the transaction wording, not country demand. [limbo seller page](https://www.limbomusic.com/es/sell-your-music-catalog), [Symphonic NEXT](https://symphoniclatino.com/next/).

**Expansion:** Larrosa uses valuation and catalog/royalty monetization language; limbo uses `valuación`. Keep both `valoración de catálogo musical` and `valuación de catálogo musical` for later ES/MX comparison. Prioritizing `valoración` in Spain and testing `valuación` in Mexico is an editorial hypothesis, not a measured regional preference. `vender regalías musicales` and `vender derechos musicales` are derived candidates with weaker exact-phrase evidence. [Larrosa](https://www.larrosa.pro/compra-venta-regalias-catalogos).

**Spain-specific evidence:** AIE's article dated 2024-12-20 discusses catalog sales in Spain and differentiates composition and recording rights. This is local industry vocabulary and context, not a conversion-intent study. Avoid a blanket claim that selling a catalog means transferring every kind of right. [AIE](https://www.aie.es/por-que-no-es-habitual-la-venta-de-catalogos-musicales-en-espana/).

**Mexico-specific limitation:** The Mexico-qualified sample did not establish a strong Mexico-specific independent-catalog buyer intake page. Casete's Mexican site is evidence of local distribution, publishing administration and royalty collection language, **not catalog purchasing**. Do not attribute the pan-Spanish results or Spain's findings to Mexico. [Casete services](https://casete.com.mx/servicios).

**Use on an eventual seller page:** identify owners of earning recordings/catalogs, explain the specific master or royalty interest under consideration, and invite a catalog review. Do not replace the sale proposition with `anticipo de regalías`, `adelanto de regalías`, `préstamo`, `vender canciones` or `distribución musical`.

## Portuguese: Brazil

**Primary cluster:** `vender catálogo musical` and `venda de catálogo musical`. The Brazil-facing Symphonic NEXT page explicitly addresses independent artists, labels and rights holders with established revenue, and distinguishes partial/full acquisitions from advances. This is the closest locally targeted offer match in the sample. [Symphonic Brazil](https://symdistro.com.br/next/).

**Expansion:** RIGHTR actually uses `valuation de catálogos musicais` and describes economic analysis for sale, licensing and investment. This supports retaining the English loanword instead of forcing a literal translation; `avaliação de catálogo musical` remains a normalized companion candidate. It is an advisory/valuation service, not proof of a direct purchasing offer. [RIGHTR](https://rightr.co/).

Delta Juliet's Portuguese page addresses master rights, publishing and royalty streams, but identifies a Danish base and European priority. Treat it as Portuguese-language direct-buyer evidence, not Brazil-only evidence. Brodr connects sellers with buyers of fractional catalogs, so `vender royalties musicais` is a derived, mixed-intent candidate rather than a clean direct-sale term. [Delta Juliet](https://www.delta-juliet.com/pt/), [Brodr](https://brodr.com/).

**Terminology trap found in expansion:** unqualified `vender direitos de master` searches surfaced master-resale-rights digital-product content as well as music. It is not selected as a primary term; specify music recordings/fonogramas in supporting copy. Broad `direitos autorais`, `licenciamento musical`, `arrecadação`, `adiantamento de royalties` and `antecipação de royalties` must not be counted wholly as catalog-sale demand.

## German: Germany

**Primary cluster:** `Musikkatalog verkaufen`. REHKATSCH has an explicit German seller page for catalogs, publishing interests and master recordings, including a local legal-advice CTA. It is strong evidence of seller-stage vocabulary, but a law firm is not a direct catalog buyer. `Masterrecordings verkaufen` comes from this provider's wording, not a literal translation of “sell masters.” [REHKATSCH](https://rehkatsch.de/rechtsgebiete/musikrecht/musikkatalog-verkaufen/).

**Expansion:** Unchained's German page title supplies the `Musikkatalog-Bewertung` family and its copy supports valuation before partial/full sales. It arranges transactions with partners and also promotes fractional royalties, so it is not proof of an identical direct-buyer model or German domicile. `Musikkatalog bewerten` is a derived query variant. [Unchained German page](https://www.unchainedmusic.io/de/rights-management/).

Bertelsmann's BMG/KKR release uses `Musikrechte verkaufen`, establishing local company usage. The underlying announcement date is **2021-03-24**, regardless of newer crawl/display metadata; it is historical evidence, not verification of a current offer. [Bertelsmann announcement](https://www.bertelsmann.com/de/news-und-media/nachrichten/bmg-und-kkr-buendeln-kraefte-zum-kauf-von-musikrechten.jsp).

**Exclude or separate:** `Musik verkaufen` (distribution/download sales), `Musikrechte kaufen` (buyers), `GEMA-freie Musik` (licensing), `Royalty-Vorschuss` (advance), and generic `Tantiemen`. Query expansion on selling royalties also surfaced distribution and beat-sale material. Do not equate the legal vocabulary “Urheberrechte” with all transferable recording/royalty interests.

## French: France

**Primary cluster:** `vendre catalogue musical` and `vente de catalogue musical`. ANote's French creator/help pages explain selling catalog-linked future royalties, providing seller-side language distinct from its investor marketplace. The interface language alone does not establish France-specific demand. [ANote creators](https://www.anotemusic.com/fr/music-creators), [ANote seller help](https://support.anotemusic.com/fr/how-can-i-list-my-music-catalogue-on-anote-music-platform).

**France-specific evidence:** Bolero identifies itself as a French company in its FAQ and separately addresses creators who own masters/publishing. Its marketplace also serves investors, and its creator offer is not interchangeable with a direct whole-catalog purchase. Use the creator-specific evidence for `vendre droits musicaux`, never the site's overall investment positioning as seller search demand. [Bolero FAQ](https://www.boleromusic.com/fr/faqs), [Bolero creators](https://www.boleromusic.com/fr/for-creators).

**Expansion:** `valorisation catalogue musical` follows native valuation wording; `vendre royalties musicales` is derived from future-royalty sale explanations. Both need qualification. France's CNMlab separately discusses music-catalog acquisitions and the distinction among rights structures, reinforcing that this is not simply general licensing. [CNMlab](https://cnmlab.fr/onde-courte/linvestissement-dans-des-catalogues-musicaux-francais-et-etrangers/).

**Not primary seller demand:** `rachat catalogue musical` is commercially relevant but leans toward acquisition/news and does not identify the searcher as a seller. `cession de droits d'auteur` includes broader contracts; `acheter droits musicaux` and `investir dans la musique` are buyer/investor intent. Do not copy competitor promises about retaining all IP or creative control without confirming Space Music's actual deal terms.

[Pophouse](https://pophouse.se/press-room/pophouse-entertainment-acquires-aviciis-back-catalogue/).

**Evidence gap:** no comparable Swedish-language independent-catalog seller intake was verified in this sample. This is not proof of absent supply, absent demand or zero search volume. Do not force unverified literal variants such as “sälja royaltyintressen” into the core list. Separate `sälja musik` (distribution), `musiklicens` (licensing), `köpa musikrättigheter` (buyers) and royalty-collection education.

## Interpretation Guardrails

1. A search-volume estimate for `direitos autorais`, `droits musicaux`, `Musikrechte`, `regalías` or `musikrättigheter` cannot be allocated wholly to catalog sales. Only a seller-qualified query cluster belongs in a commercial demand estimate.
2. Advances and recoupable funding may be alternatives a seller compares, but they are not the requested product. Keep them in comparison/supporting content, not the primary acquisition promise.
3. Marketplace “sell” can mean an investor reselling purchased shares. Seller ownership must mean the original artist/label/rightsholder interest relevant to Space Music, not a retail investment position.
4. Catalog-sale news supports terminology, not the number of likely sellers. A competitor landing page proves an offer, not that anyone searches for it.
5. Masters, composition/publishing interests, royalty receivables and other rights are not interchangeable. This is intent research, not legal or valuation advice. Do not import legal assertions, minimum earnings, valuation multiples or completion timelines from competitors.
6. Future numeric validation should preserve language, country database, exact keyword, retrieval date, metric snapshot/period and returned volume/KD separately. A missing row, access error, unavailable KD and explicit numeric 0 are four different states.
7. None of the evidence supports a genre restriction. Qualify by ownership and existing earnings rather than borrowing a competitor's genre examples.
