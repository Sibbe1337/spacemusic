## Evidence Handling

- All filenames in the tables refer to the `raw/` directory in this folder. The machine-readable raw index supplies absolute paths.
- Exact request parameters and actual result payloads are preserved; no raw response has been rewritten into a fabricated report. The surrounding record adds an ID, tool name and retrieval timestamp.
- Results include irrelevant material, translated pages and snippets from other countries. Such inclusion is part of the audit trail, not endorsement or reliance.
- Provider offer descriptions support their own positioning, not independent confirmation of claims, deal outcomes, compliance, funding, or customer counts.
- Search-result order, number of results, article age and crawl recency are not keyword metrics.
- Parent-reported US/English Planner figures are outside these target language-country pairs and are not imported. Parent's GSC country observations are site performance, not keyword volume or language attribution.
- Parent supplied Germany/German and Sweden/Swedish Planner observations for September 2025-August 2026, Google only. The report includes a separately attributed addendum and `raw/parent-planner-update.json` preserves the parent message. This worker did not independently inspect that UI evidence.
