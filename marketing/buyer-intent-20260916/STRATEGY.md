# SPACE: high-intent catalog acquisition search

September 16, 2026. Observations, recommendations and implemented preview changes are separated below. This is a bounded first batch, not a claim to cover every search or to have completed the site's factual audit. Nothing here is a production release.

## Business brief

SPACE is a potential direct purchaser of existing revenue-generating music catalog rights or royalty interests. The intended sellers are independent artists, producers, labels and catalog owners across genres. The commercial problem is access to an upfront payment in exchange for a precisely defined interest, while understanding the income, control and duties that remain. A label funding a new release can consider selling existing catalog interests, but this is not a SPACE loan, advance or credit facility.

The application and initial review are free, with no listing fee, auction commission or obligation to accept an offer. A submitted form is not an approval, appraisal or guaranteed purchase. The primary action is `/apply/`, with its existing Typeform flow and explicit Formspree alternative retained. Sources: [SPACE offer policy](https://spacemusic.io/pricing.md), [Apply](https://spacemusic.io/apply/) and the owner's approved all-genre direction.

Language: English. Service positioning is international, but actual country exclusions and transaction eligibility remain to be confirmed operationally. US is the first proposed keyword-research market because it led the historical Search Console traffic, not because all users or transactions are American. UK and other markets need separate data. Do not create city/country pages or claim offices and local eligibility without evidence.

## Measurement and release gates

100 Google Web clicks/day means 2,800 clicks across 28 complete days, not 100 daily impressions. It is a target, not a forecast; it is roughly 54 times the old baseline's daily average. Track 5, 15, 30, 60 and 100 average daily clicks as checkpoints, with no promised dates.

The business outcome remains unique qualified applications, with contactability, rights and documented income manually reviewed. Keep unreviewed leads separate. Track page/source and genre only using approved systems; never send financial amounts, names, email, catalog identifiers or documents to public analytics.

Before production: confirm fresh baseline or explicitly accept the measurement gap; run comparable mobile checks; verify the complete Apply navigation/flow regression set without test leads; review all changed copy and ensure preview noindex is not inherited by production. The old 33 Apply checks and a fresh three-run mobile LCP comparison have not been claimed for this batch. Investigate a median LCP regression above both 10% and 250 ms before promotion.

Let T be the production publish date. At T+28 compare T+1 through T+28 with T-28 through T-1, omitting the partial release day. Repeat with equal complete 56-day periods. Use the same search type, exact canonical page, query cohort, country and device filters; separate branded queries. Reconcile accepted application events with receiver records and report conversions separately from search clicks. Changes in rankings or leads are measured outcomes; content quality, seasonality and changing demand are possible explanations, not proof of causation.
