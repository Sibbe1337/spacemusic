## Is a royalty advance the same as a catalog loan?

Not necessarily. A product called an advance can be structured as a purchase of specified future receipts rather than a loan. For example, [beatBread describes its advances](https://www.beatbread.com/faq) as limited revenue purchases, and publishes a separate [label-funding offering](https://www.beatbread.com/label). Those are that provider's descriptions, not SPACE products. Read the recoupment, duration, underperformance and collection provisions of the actual offer.

## A label funding decision starts with retained cash flow

For a release campaign, artist signing or operating budget, identify the cash shortfall and payment dates first. Then distinguish the label's income from money owed to artists and producers. Selling the label's interest is not permission to sell another rights holder's share.

Hypothetical example: a selected catalog produces $2,000 a month for the label after attributable deductions and third-party payouts. A sale of 50% of that label income interest would leave $1,000 a month if receipts stayed unchanged. Whether the purchase price meets a release budget is a separate question. This is not a valuation, offer or forecast.

For the complete preparation workflow, see [independent label funding options](/blog/independent-record-label-funding-options/). If selling part of the existing catalog is worth considering, [request a free review](/apply/). SPACE does not provide a loan or guarantee that a sale will cover your budget.

## Compare these points in each written proposal

- Rights and recordings included, ownership share, territory, duration and future-release exclusions.
- Cash payable after fees, outstanding balances and closing conditions.
- Future royalty collections, income retained and obligations if earnings are lower than expected.
- Security, guarantees, recoupment, repayment, early-exit terms and any continuing reporting duties.
- Consents, artist payments, warranties, reserved approvals and independent professional costs.

Use the [offer checklist](/blog/music-catalog-offer-checklist/) before signing. No single multiple, interest rate or percentage establishes which route is suitable.
