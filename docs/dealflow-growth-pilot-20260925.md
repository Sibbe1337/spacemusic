# SPACE: brokerage-first pilot and acquisition drafts

Status: implementation preview, not a launch announcement. 25 September 2026.
User decision: both brokerage and eventual buyer access, starting with brokerage.

## Product implemented in this increment

A submitted case can receive a versioned human review, source references and
documented buyer criteria. The reviewer prepares a recipient-specific package.
The owner can approve, refuse or revoke that exact version. Missing/conflicting
evidence, incompatible rights, stale criteria and currency differences block
automatic readiness. New evidence invalidates old preparation and permissions.

The software does not sell contact details, send the package, set a price or
collect commission. No customer data is sent to Jev. Existing website content,
Apply, Typeform delivery and SEO are unchanged. The backend capability is off by
default and production composition has not been enabled or deployed.

## First real transactions: requirements before enabling

- Confirm the legal operator and named operational/restore owner, as already
  required for the customer pilot. Existing infrastructure/privacy gates remain.
- Decide and disclose SPACE's role per transaction: principal buyer or seller
  intermediary. Do not silently change a direct-purchase promise into brokerage.
- Obtain a reviewed seller mandate covering scope, excluded future music, term,
  fee basis, payment trigger, conflicts and recipient-specific sharing. The
  presentation checkbox is not a signed brokerage mandate or sale agreement.
- Establish actual buyer contacts and decision-makers and confirm their current
  criteria. No invented names, budgets, verified funds or inferred interest.
- Review the selected documents before disclosure, including unrelated third
  parties and confidentiality restrictions. A selected filename is not redaction.
- Approve an authenticated staging rollout and real mail/file-delivery design
  separately. Keep production closed and the existing five-case pilot limit.

Initially, use an agreed fee arrangement per completed transaction rather than
claiming a published tariff or automatically billing leads. No fee percentage,
minimum catalog value, revenue forecast or income guarantee has been set here.
Buyer subscriptions require demonstrated recurring value and their own product,
consent and access-control work; they are not enabled by this increment.

## Acquisition drafts, in order

### 1. Help existing applicants supply the next missing item

Where: the existing private case conversation, not a bulk marketing list.
Trigger: an assigned reviewer identifies a missing document or unresolved scope.
Draft message for human adaptation:

> Thank you for your catalog submission. To continue the review, please provide
> [specific missing item] in your existing workspace. We need this to establish
> [specific question]. Please do not include unrelated personal or financial
> records. Your future releases remain outside the proposed scope unless you
> explicitly ask us to consider a separate transaction.

Resolve the placeholders before sending. No automatic follow-up or response-time
promise. Record missing information separately from an unsuitable catalog.

### 2. Test referrals from a small number of relevant partners

Audience: managers, label administrators and royalty accountants already known
to SPACE. Do not purchase or import private contact lists.
Draft explanation for individual outreach, after the operating model is approved:

> If a client is considering a sale of existing music rights, SPACE can review
> the scope and supporting royalty records. The rights owner applies directly
> at https://spacemusic.io/apply/. If a potential introduction is appropriate,
> we first agree our role and obtain permission for a named recipient and the
> specific information to share. Please do not send us a client's confidential
> statements or contact details without their permission.

Do not promise referral compensation until its terms are separately agreed.
Measure applications actually received and manually reviewed, not email opens.

## Measurement and decisions

Use equal complete 28-day windows with a saved cutoff and the same attribution
rules. The new interface does not claim to have measured these figures.

| Metric | Evidence | Important distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Received applications | Durable source response reference | Not a browser button click |
| Reviewed applications | Named human review with date | Unreviewed is not rejected |
| Ready for preparation | Current evidence and recorded buyer criteria | Not verified ownership or buyer interest |
| Approved presentations | Exact package and owner decision | Not an actual introduction |
| Actual introductions | Confirmed human-sent introduction/delivery record | No delivery implemented here |
| Offers and completed deals | Documented offer/closing evidence | No automatic valuation |
| SPACE revenue | Actual fee entitlement and payment record | Not modeled deal value |

Compare document-completion rate and review time first. Do not buy more traffic
or launch buyer subscriptions because a small synthetic test looks successful.
Review real pilot cases before choosing the next acquisition experiment.
