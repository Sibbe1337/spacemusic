What Is a Music Catalog Valuation Memo?

A music catalog valuation memo is a formal investment-grade document that establishes the fair market value of a recorded music or publishing catalog. It is the primary deliverable used by artists, label executives, managers, and institutional buyers when negotiating catalog acquisitions, royalty-backed financing, or estate planning. Unlike a simple earnings multiple, a rigorous valuation memo accounts for streaming decay curves, territory-level performance, distributor concentration risk, and forward revenue scenarios under different market conditions.

SPACE produces 19-slide 16:9 institutional memos powered by a 10,000-path Monte Carlo discounted cash flow (DCF) engine, Dynamic Time Warping (DTW) decay matching against a library of comparable catalog trajectories, and TypeSafe AI (JEV) forensic verification of distributor statement data. The result is a credible, audit-ready document that meets the standards expected by music investment funds, private equity buyers, and royalty-advance lenders.
