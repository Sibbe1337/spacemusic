Music Rights Glossary

Plain-language definitions of music rights, royalty, and catalog terms for independent artists and catalog owners.

Ownership & Rights

  • Master Rights — Ownership of the original sound recording.
  • Publishing Rights — Ownership of the underlying musical composition.
  • Music Publisher — Entity that administers publishing rights on behalf of songwriters.
  • Record Label — Company that owns or licenses master recordings.
  • Independent Artist — Artist who owns and controls their own rights.
  • Neighboring Rights — Rights of performers and producers in sound recordings, separate from composition rights.

Royalties

  • Mechanical Royalties — Fees paid when a composition is reproduced (streams, downloads, physical).
  • Performance Royalties — Fees collected by PROs when music is publicly performed or broadcast.
  • Streaming Royalties — Revenue generated from plays on digital streaming platforms.
  • Royalty Split — How royalty income is divided among rights holders.
  • PRO — Performing Rights Organization; collects and distributes performance royalties.

Catalog & Valuation

  • Music Catalog — A collection of music rights an artist, label, or publisher owns.
  • Catalog Valuation — Process of determining the market value of a music catalog.
  • Revenue Multiple — A valuation method that multiplies annual net income by a factor to estimate sale price.
  • Catalog Acquisition — Purchase of a music catalog by a buyer.
  • Buyout Deal — A transaction in which all rights are transferred to the buyer for a lump-sum payment.
  • Partial Sale vs Full Sale — Selling a share of rights vs. transferring the entire catalog.
  • Catalog Loan — Borrowing against catalog revenue without transferring ownership.

Licensing & Deals

  • Sync Licensing — Licensing music for use in film, TV, ads, and video games.
  • Blanket License — A license granting rights to an entire catalog rather than individual tracks.
  • Compulsory License — A statutory right to use a work without negotiating directly with the rights holder.
  • Exclusive vs Non-Exclusive License — Whether a licensee is the only party permitted to use the rights.
  • Distribution Deal — Agreement covering the distribution of recordings to stores and streaming platforms.
  • Sub-Publishing — Licensing publishing rights to a local publisher in a specific territory.
  • Sample Clearance — Permission to use an existing recording or composition in a new work.

Advances & Recoupment

  • Advance — Upfront payment recouped from future royalties.
  • Recoupment — The process by which an advance is paid back from earned royalties.

Metadata & Identifiers

  • Metadata — Descriptive data embedded in or associated with music files.
  • ISRC — International Standard Recording Code; unique ID for each sound recording.
  • ISWC — International Standard Musical Work Code; unique ID for each composition.
  • Content ID — YouTube's system for identifying and monetizing copyrighted content.
  • Cue Sheet — Document listing music used in a film or TV production, filed with PROs.
  • Digital Aggregator — Service that distributes music to streaming platforms on behalf of artists.
