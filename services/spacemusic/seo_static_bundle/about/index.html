About SPACE

SPACE is a direct buyer of revenue-generating music catalogs from independent artists, producers, and labels. We consider all genres and provide individual, documented reviews — not automated offers.

What we do

We acquire master and publishing rights directly, without an auction or listing marketplace. Our two-stage underwriting process gives catalog owners a clear, defensible valuation based on documented income.

Direct principal acquisition offers from SPACE carry a 0% seller platform fee. Optional syndicated placement with our institutional buyer network is available for larger catalogs.

Our approach

  • Stage 1 — Scenario Review: Explore an indicative range via our free calculator and submit an initial application. No upload required to start.
  • Stage 2 — Statement-Verified Underwriting: Upload 12–36 months of distributor or PRO CSV statements. Our DCF and Dynamic Time Warping (DTW) forensics engine produces a verified offer, typically within 5–7 business days.
  • 11-Point Forensics: Every statement-verified catalog goes through SPACE's proprietary fraud and integrity checks before an offer is issued.

Who we work with

Independent artists, producers, record labels, and catalog owners across Hip-Hop, R&B, Pop, Rock, Electronic, Ambient, Classical, Latin, and all other genres. Income, rights, and documentation are reviewed individually. An application does not guarantee an offer.
