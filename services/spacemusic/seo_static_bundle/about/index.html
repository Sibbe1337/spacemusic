About SPACE

SPACE is a direct buyer of revenue-generating music catalogs. We work with independent artists, producers, and labels across every genre — from hip-hop and R&B to ambient, classical, and electronic — to help catalog owners understand what their music is worth and, when the fit is right, make a clear and direct offer to acquire it.

Our Mission

Most independent creators have spent years building catalogs that generate consistent royalty income. Yet the process of selling that catalog — if they choose to — has historically been opaque, slow, and tilted toward institutional buyers with more information than the seller.

SPACE was founded to change that. We believe creators deserve a direct, transparent path to liquidity for the music they own. No auction. No third-party broker taking a percentage. No black-box valuations. Just a straightforward review of your rights and income, followed by a clear offer if your catalog is a fit.

We review every catalog individually. An application is not a guarantee, but it is a genuine starting point — and it costs you nothing to find out where you stand.

What We Do

SPACE acquires revenue-generating music catalogs directly from the people who own them. That means we are the buyer, not a marketplace or a broker. When we make an offer, it is a principal offer — we are the counterparty, and there is no auction process or third-party commission on a direct acquisition.

We consider catalogs across all genres. Hip-hop, R&B, pop, rock, country, Latin, ambient, lo-fi, electronic, house, techno, classical, jazz, meditation, sleep music, wellness soundscapes — every genre with documented royalty income is eligible to apply. We review masters, publishing rights, or a defined share of either, depending on what the owner holds and is willing to sell.

Our review process is built around documented income. We look at royalty statements, streaming history, ownership documentation, and rights agreements to build an individual picture of each catalog's value. No two catalogs are the same, and we do not apply a fixed formula.

How the Process Works

  1. Start with a free review request. Share the basics about your catalog — your name, the music you own, and any royalty income you receive. You do not need documents at this stage. The intake takes a few minutes.
  2. We review the evidence privately. After you apply, we follow up by email to confirm the specific income and rights documents we need. Everything happens through a confidential process. We check ownership, rights history, income trends, and metadata.
  3. Receive a clear next step. If your catalog is a fit, we return a structured offer within 5–7 business days of receiving the relevant information. There is no obligation to accept. You can review the offer with an independent adviser before making any decision.

Who We Work With

We work with independent artists who have been releasing music for years and have built a catalog with steady streaming or sync income. We work with producers who have placed tracks on platforms or licensed music to media. We work with independent labels that hold rights to multiple artists or projects. We work with catalog owners who inherited or acquired rights and are exploring their options.

The common thread is documented income from music rights — streaming royalties, sync fees, neighboring rights, or publishing revenue. If your music earns money and you own the rights to sell, we want to hear from you.

Why Creators Choose SPACE

The most common reason creators come to SPACE is clarity. They want to know what their catalog is actually worth to a real buyer, not a hypothetical auction estimate. Our process gives them that answer without requiring them to commit to a sale.

The second reason is simplicity. There are no listing fees to apply. No brokers taking a percentage of the deal. No years-long negotiation process. We make one direct offer based on our review, and the creator decides what to do with it.

The third reason is respect. We do not publish catalogs on an open marketplace or share information with buyers you did not ask to hear from. Everything about the review process is private, and your information stays between you and SPACE unless you choose otherwise.
