## Design Context

### Users
Independent artists, producers and catalog owners evaluating a direct music
catalog sale, learning about rights, and submitting a confidential application.
The owner wants more qualified leads and a less fragmented experience.

### Brand Personality
Clear, direct, credible. Explain what SPACE buys and make the next step obvious
without pressure, visual gimmicks or competing calls to action.

### Aesthetic Direction
The owner explicitly requested Inter earlier in this conversation, rejected
generic AI-looking colors and typography, and wants less visual noise. Retain
SPACE's identity. The implementation direction is a restrained editorial layout,
navy ink, cool paper surfaces and accessible blue actions.

### Design Principles
1. Preserve every existing text, section, link, route and SEO signal.
2. Improve hierarchy and navigation rather than shortening the content.
3. Use one readable type system across marketing, application and workspace.
4. Design for mobile, keyboard navigation, zoom and reduced motion.
5. Build and test a separate preview before changing production.
