# SPACE: Corrected Competitor Analysis and Priorities

Reviewed: 2026-09-07. Input: the supplied analysis dated 2026-09-03.
Status: internal decision support, not public website copy. No production
changes or deployments are part of this review.

## Executive Decision

Keep the direct-purchase proposition, but do not build SPACE's positioning
on supposedly absent competitors, guaranteed speed, higher prices or unique
partial sales. The supplied report contains material factual errors and
treats several untested hypotheses as established advantages.

**Strategic assessment:** Duetti and Limbo deserve first attention for
purchase-proposal comparisons. Royalty Exchange is a marketplace alternative;
beatBread competes for creators who want cash while preserving underlying
ownership. SellYourMusicRights competes for educational search attention,
not demonstrably for the same acquisitions. Primary Wave is a useful
established-catalog benchmark, not proof that all independent creators are
excluded from institutional buyers.

The immediate priority is consistency and evidence on existing pages, not
another batch of overlapping comparison articles.

## Sequential Action Plan

### 1. Align Existing Claims

Correct the three pages above before creating another generic alternatives
article. Start with factual body-copy changes, keeping existing paths,
canonicals and metadata identity. Any inaccurate metadata must be reviewed
explicitly rather than preserved blindly. Verify rendered and static text
together, plus application links and the sitemap, before deployment.

Acceptance: no unsupported claims of guaranteed acceptance, superior prices,
universal genre exclusion, undisclosed fees or payment before transfer
conditions. The new guides and the legacy pages must agree on the facts.

### 2. Replace Claimed Advantages with Evidence

Only publish completed-transaction examples with verified records and the
seller's permission. Useful proof is rights/share sold, revenue period,
elapsed time to a conditional offer and time to payment, with those stages
clearly distinguished. Never invent testimonials, transaction counts,
accepted offers or performance benchmarks to fill a design component.

If there are too few completed transactions, show the real review process,
document checklist and exclusions instead. Genre expertise is a positioning
hypothesis until supported by actual work and outcomes, not proof that
other buyers undervalue that genre.

### 3. Measure Qualified Leads, Not Just Clicks

Define one funnel from landing page to application, qualified application,
offer and accepted offer. Deduplicate leads internally and exclude known
tests/spam. Keep personal contact details and royalty documents out of
analytics events. Compare landing page, genre and period consistently.

The decision metric should be qualified applications per relevant visit,
with absolute counts and later offer acceptance alongside it. No uplift
percentage or test duration is justified without baseline volume. With
small samples, report uncertainty and individual friction findings rather
than declaring a conversion winner.

### 4. Expand Genre Content Based on Evidence

Select the next genre from Search Console queries and qualified lead data,
not the claim that no competitors exist. Improve an existing canonical
page where possible. Add a new page only for a distinct seller question
that SPACE can answer with real expertise or examples.

Keep the main proposition broad and simple while genre pages supply detail.
Broad acquisition review must not become a promise to buy every catalog.
Retain useful existing URLs and internal links during each small release.

## Positioning Direction

Recommended message, not a deployed headline:

SPACE is a direct buyer for independent catalog owners. Request a free
review of a defined share or full catalog, receive a proposal based on the
rights and records, and decide whether it fits your plans. There is no
auction or obligation to accept an offer.

Lead with the transaction and seller choice. Support the process with actual
evidence. Avoid "we pay most," "only buyer for your genre," and unqualified
"cash in seven days."

## Method and Limits

This is a public-product-description quick scan, not a contract review,
private-offer test, mystery-shopping exercise or quantitative market study.
Company statements establish published positioning, not independently
verified operational performance.

No DataForSEO or Firecrawl connector was available. Primary websites were
read through web retrieval and public HTTP requests. No paid SEO dataset,
backlink counts, keyword volumes, current Search Console export, fresh
Plausible export or customer interview data was used. None of those missing
metrics is treated as zero or inferred from company size.

Raw HTML/text responses and retrieval metadata were saved under
`raw/<company>/2026-09-07/scrapes/` before synthesis, including six SPACE
pages. Each response has its URL, HTTP status, timestamp and SHA-256 hash.
The archive contains source material, not approved claims or instructions.
