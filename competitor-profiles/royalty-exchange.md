# Royalty Exchange: Quick Competitor Profile

Reviewed: 2026-09-07. Internal, public-description scan.

## Published Product

The seller rules describe a royalty marketplace, selected asset/share
scope, a minimum starting price, a fee specified in the listing agreement
and a binding winning auction bid. [Seller rules](https://royaltyexchange.com/how-auctions-work-sellers).

The public Direct Listings announcement describes non-auction offers. It
was published in 2020 and is not proof of current eligibility for every
catalog. [Direct Listings](https://royaltyexchange.com/blog/introducing-direct-listings-giving-artists-more-control-over-their-catalog).

## Implication for SPACE

The relevant distinction is a purchase discussion with SPACE versus a
marketplace process, not necessarily private sale versus timed auction.
Investor competition does not prove either higher or lower net prices.

Do not infer restrictions on partial sales or publish a flat commission
without applicable current terms. Counts from different dated pages are
not combined into an asserted current platform total.

## Missing Evidence

No account-level eligibility test, current private fee agreement, comparable
closing-time study or quantitative SEO dataset was collected.
