# beatBread: Quick Competitor Profile

Reviewed: 2026-09-07. Internal, public-description scan.

## Published Product

beatBread describes a limited revenue purchase, not a loan. Its FAQ lists
a $278 plus 2.8% fee collected through future revenue, with no interest.
Collections can extend beyond the term if unrecouped. [FAQ](https://www.beatbread.com/faq).

The artist-funding page distinguishes estimates from confirmed offers and
allows choices over retained income and new releases. Full income returns
after both recoupment and term completion. [Artist funding](https://www.beatbread.com/artist-funding).

## Implication for SPACE

Treat it as an alternative for access to capital, not an inferior permanent
sale. Which transaction fits depends on ownership, duration, available cash
and continuing income. Compare the seller's objective before comparing a
headline amount.

No universal 1.5-3x multiple, 6-30 month term or superior SPACE payout was
established. Non-recourse language is not permission to ignore contractual
obligations.

## Missing Evidence

No private offer, signed terms, payout test, traffic or keyword metrics were
collected.
