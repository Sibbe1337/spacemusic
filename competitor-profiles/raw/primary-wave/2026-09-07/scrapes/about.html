Who We Are

Primary Wave Music is the leading independent publisher of iconic and legendary music in the world, setting the standard for excellence in music publishing. Home to some of the most influential songwriters, artists, and record labels in the history of recorded music, our catalog spans generations and includes over 1,000 Top 10 singles and more than 400 number one hits.

As trusted partners to artists and estates, we steward and elevate these extraordinary bodies of work with a long-term vision, ensuring their cultural relevance endures across generations. Our approach is rooted in an entrepreneurial spirit and a deep respect for the music, combining strategic investment, creative marketing, and tailored services to unlock new opportunities for every catalog we represent.

We focus on honoring legacy while driving future impact, reintroducing timeless music to new audiences and creating new pathways for iconic catalogs to live, grow, and resonate in a modern marketplace.

Larry Mestel

Founder & CEO

Lawrence Mestel, Founder & CEO of Primary Wave Music, has been working in the music and entertainment industry for over 30 years. In 2006, Mestel took his extensive experience working at multiple record labels and founded Primary Wave Music, one of the largest independent music publishing, marketing and talent management companies in the United States. Primary Wave has controlled over 50,000 songs from the catalogs of legendary artists such as Bob Marley, Prince, Stevie Nicks, James Brown, Sun Records, Frankie Valli & the Four Seasons, Robby Krieger and Ray Manzarek (The Doors), Smokey Robinson, Whitney Houston, Burt Bacharach, Bing Crosby, Luther Vandross, Olivia Newton-John, Ray Charles, Aerosmith, Def Leppard, America, War, Gerry Goffin, Robbie Robertson, Traveling Wilburys (Bob Dylan), Teddy Pendergrass, Count Basie, Sly & The Family Stone, Boston, Alice Cooper, Paul Anka, Boy George and Culture Club, Allee Willis, Leon Russell, Paul Rodgers, Free, Toots & The Maytals, Steve Cropper, Glenn Gould, Air Supply, Huey Lewis and the News, Alice In Chains, Devo, Donny Hathaway, Dan Wilson, Chris Isaak, KT Tunstall, Sturken & Rogers, and many more. Originally founded on music publishing, Mestel has strategically and successfully built the company into a full-service music and entertainment powerhouse offering in-house branding, digital marketing, publicity, TV & Film development and a talent management company.

Mestel was one of the producers behind the critically acclaimed Kurt Cobain documentary film, Kurt Cobain: Montage of Heck and he was part of the team who produced the Whitney Houston biopic, I Wanna Dance With Somebody distributed by Sony Pictures.

In 2019 Goldman Sachs honored Larry Mestel as one of the “100 most intriguing entrepreneurs” at the annual Goldman Sachs Builders & Inventors summit. In 2020, and then again in 2022 & 2023, Mestel landed on Billboard Magazine’s “Power List” being recognized for his leadership in the music industry. In 2024, Mestel was once again featured on the Billboard Power 100, making the magazine’s “Leaderboard” which recognized the top 40 leaders across the music industry He also appeared on the Variety 500 twice and has been featured in top press outlets such as New York Times, Los Angeles Times, Wall Street Journal, The Hollywood Reporter, Entrepreneur, Fast Company, New York Post, Forbes, Billboard Magazine, and HITS Magazine.

Prior to founding Primary Wave Music, Larry Mestel was Chief Operating Officer and General Manager of Virgin Records. From 2000 – 2004, Mestel was Executive Vice President and General Manager at Arista Records. Mestel also held the position of Chief Operating Officer for Island Entertainment Group, which included Island Records, Island Music Publishing and Island Pictures.

Justin Shukat

President

Justin Shukat is a partner and President of Primary Wave Music Publishing since 2006. He oversees all areas of Primary Wave’s creative team including synch and A&R while working alongside all partner companies to ensure that all businesses are focused and delivering. He has been responsible for new writer signings and multiple catalog acquisitions and is now focused on working across the music industry to inspire and facilitate recordings that derive from the timeless music aggregated within the Primary Wave music IP portfolio. Given the income potential of a new current hit, this is a highly impactful, efficient and profitable initiative.

Justin’s music career began at a young age, as he was lucky enough to grow up learning, listening, and experiencing from his father and mentor, the legendary entertainment attorney Peter Shukat. His musical journey started with working with the Montreux Jazz Festival in the mid 90’s followed by various positions at Walter Yetnikoff’s Velvel Records. Shukat then moved on to senior marketing positions at both Arista and Epic Records. In 2005 he re-connected with Larry Mestel who had a vision and plan during a transitional time in the music business to launch a new company focused on acquiring and working legacy catalogs and together they started Primary Wave. Justin has been recognized by Billboard Magazine as an industry Power Player (2023 & 2022) as well as being included in the 40 Under 40 in 2013. Mr. Shukat received a BA in Marketing from the Martin J. Whitman School of Management at Syracuse University in 1996.

At Primary Wave, Natalia is responsible for development of unique and diversified content strategies for the company’s legendary and iconic artists and song catalogues.

Jeff Straughn

Chief Branding Officer

“Regrets, I’ve had a few, but then again, too few to mention”

“My Way” – Paul Anka

With over thirty years of core marketing experience, Jeff Straughn worked for global advertising agencies before transitioning to sports, entertainment and music where he ran the partnership group at global powerhouse Octagon.

By the early 2000s, Straughn had leveraged his brand experience and was hired by and reported directly to Mr, Shawn “Jay-Z” Carter and Mr. Antonio “LA” Reid. Straughn was quickly challenged to build a new strategic marketing department for Island Records and Def Jam Recordings (IDJ) that would help generate new income in support of their artists and new releases. Straughn was a senior member of the leadership team at IDJ where he quickly became a trendsetter in how brands and artists work together, creating disruptive and creative concepts supporting the artist and their new work.

In 2008, Straughn met his current partner and the founder of Primary Wave, Larry Mestel. Collectively, they built the blueprint for Primary Wave’s brand strategy. The goal was simple: build a team to support both artist management clients as well as the growing and diverse music publishing catalog. Additionally, Straughn and Mestel set up a branding agency known as Brand Synergy Group to serve as a consultant for third-party brands in the entertainment/music space.

Since joining Primary Wave, Straughn has developed and executed a multitude of brand partnerships through a combination of traditional endorsements, private performances, and a breakthrough of new intellectual properties that leverage Primary Wave’s copyrights and/or the artist brand ID. Each of these opportunities result in equity that is co-owned by Primary Wave and the other parties.

Straughn seeks innovative programs to deliver a win for the brand, the artist and Primary Wave. “We look at all sides of the equation as we develop each concept” says Straughn, “no two programs are ever the same.” Primary Wave believes that any and all partnerships must be results driven and are measured against a set of KPIs that are mutually beneficial for all parties. As a result, Straughn has established a long-standing list of brand partners that continues to expand and evolve as the company grows.

Additionally, for over 12 years, Straughn has leveraged his vast marketing and experiential skills to raise the Primary Wave brand profile. Each year, Straughn has built a premium VIP Primary Wave branded hospitality event during the Grammy’s. In 2019, Straughn raised the bar again by producing a full week activation and rebranding an elite hotel in West Hollywood to One Love Hotel in an effort to celebrate 75 years of Bob Marley, a marquee catalog co-owned by Primary Wave and the Marley family.
Straughn currently resides in Austin, Texas, where he moved in 2018 after spending his entire career in the northeast.

Straughn is married to a lovely wife and has two children currently in college. When not at work, he finds his passion in the kitchen, cooking and exploring new varietals to build on his wine collection.

20th Anniversary of Culture, Catalogs & Legacy

The evening featured live performances by: John Mellencamp, Pete Townshend, Joe Elliott & Phil Collen (Def Leppard), Leo Sayer and Trombone Shorty.

Through continued artist partnerships, corporate growth, and innovative marketing initiatives, 2009 reinforced Primary Wave’s reputation as a forward-thinking music company focused on maximizing the cultural and commercial value of iconic catalogs.

2008

Catalog Expansion & Brand Activation

  • In 2008, Primary Wave continued its rapid growth through the acquisition and management of iconic songwriter and artist catalogs, including partnerships with Steven Tyler (Aerosmith), Graham Parker, Chicago as well as entering into a co-publishing venture with Grammy Award-winning producer Matt Serletic and his company, Emblem Music Group.
  • The company further strengthened its reputation for innovative catalog marketing through a series of high-profile brand partnerships and sync campaigns. Highlights included the development of a limited-edition Converse Chuck Taylor sneaker inspired by the lyrics of Kurt Cobain, the release of Guitar Hero: Aerosmith—the first title in the franchise dedicated to a single artist—and a national advertising campaign featuring Shwayze’s “Buzzin'” for Pontiac.
  • Together, these initiatives reinforced Primary Wave’s strategy of transforming classic music assets into culturally relevant brands through innovative marketing, licensing, and consumer partnerships.

2007

Iconic Catalog Growth & Industry Leadership

  • In 2007, Primary Wave significantly expanded its publishing portfolio through strategic investments in both living legends and iconic estates. Major songwriter and artist partnerships included John Oates, Julian Lennon (including publishing interests in songs by The Beatles), Maurice White, Daniel Johnston, Holly Knight, Six Degrees Records, Justin Furstenfeld (Blue October) and Lamont Dozier, further establishing Primary Wave as a leader in heritage catalog acquisition and management.
  • During this period, the company helped pioneer the modern active catalog marketing model, leveraging sync licensing, brand partnerships, advertising, film, and television placements to introduce classic songs to new generations of fans.
  • Primary Wave also expanded its corporate footprint by opening a new New York office at 275 Madison Avenue and launched what would become a signature industry tradition by hosting its first annual Pre-Grammy Party at the SLS Hotel in Beverly Hills.

2006

Foundation of a New Music Model & First Landmark Acquisitions

  • Primary Wave Music Publishing is founded in April 2006 by industry veteran Larry Mestel with a transformative vision: artist-centric partnerships over traditional catalog ownership. From the outset, the company focused on actively revitalizing legacy music through modern marketing, sync, licensing, and cultural storytelling — redefining what music publishing could be.
  • The company completes its first landmark acquisition, securing 50% of Kurt Cobain’s publishing share of the Nirvana catalog. The deal establishes Primary Wave’s core acquisition strategy and sets the tone for a culture-first, value-creation approach to iconic music.
  • Acquires the MGM/United Artists publishing catalog, expanding the company’s footprint into film and classic entertainment music assets.
  • Justin Shukat joins Primary Wave as Partner.
