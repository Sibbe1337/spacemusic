The process

How it works.

Five steps from application to payment. No auctions, no investors, no hidden fees.

1

Submit your catalog

Share your streaming links, revenue data, and rights information. Takes about 10 minutes.

2

We analyze and value

Our team evaluates your streaming performance, growth trajectory, and rights structure. You get an offer within 5–7 business days.

3

Rights and metadata check

A quick verification of ISRC codes and rights ownership. We handle the paperwork.

4

Sign the agreement

Review and e-sign a clear, straightforward contract. No hidden clauses. No surprises.

5

Receive payment

Payment arrives via bank transfer when the agreement is complete. You keep creative control of future work.
