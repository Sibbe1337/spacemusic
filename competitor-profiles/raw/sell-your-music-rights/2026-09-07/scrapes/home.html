Know What Your Music Catalog Is Worth

SellYourMusicRights gives songwriters, artists, producers, and rights holders a free, instant music catalog valuation based on real market multiples from 2024-2026 catalog deals. Whether you own publishing rights, master recordings, or both, our valuation calculator shows you a realistic price range before you ever talk to a buyer or broker.

Selling music rights is a major financial decision. Publishing catalogs typically trade at 10x-18x annual net publisher share, and master rights at 12x-13x net label share, but the multiple your catalog commands depends on genre, catalog age, revenue trend, and deal structure. Our expert guides walk you through the entire process - from preparing your catalog for sale and understanding valuation methods to negotiating the best deal.
