# Primary Wave: Quick Competitor Profile

Reviewed: 2026-09-07. Internal, public-description scan.

## Published Positioning

Primary Wave emphasizes established music catalogs and partnerships with
artists and estates, combining investment, marketing and catalog services.
[About Primary Wave](https://primarywave.com/about/).

The [services page](https://primarywave.com/services/) also describes creative
development of contemporary writers and artists. Do not describe the business
as exclusively legacy acquisitions or confuse a writer-development service
with an open purchase offer for every small catalog.

## Implication for SPACE

Treat it as an established-catalog positioning benchmark. Lower immediate
relevance to a small-catalog purchase journey is an assessment, not a
verified eligibility prohibition.

The reviewed material does not establish a universal minimum deal size or
a rule excluding every independent artist. Large public transactions do
not prove either restriction. No celebrity deal amount is needed to explain
this difference in positioning.

## Missing Evidence

No private acquisition policy, representative small-catalog sample, quote,
traffic or keyword dataset was collected.
